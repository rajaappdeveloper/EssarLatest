Who We Are

About Our Company

Established in 2007, Essar Engineering Pte. Ltd. is a Singapore-based engineering and construction company delivering integrated solutions across civil, structural, architectural, mechanical, and industrial sectors. What began as a small fabrication workshop has evolved into a trusted engineering partner known for quality workmanship, technical expertise, and reliable project delivery across Singapore's built environment and industrial landscape.

With a team of experienced engineers, project managers, supervisors, and skilled tradesmen, Essar Engineering provides a comprehensive range of services including general building works, civil and structural construction, steel fabrication and erection, structural strengthening, architectural works, building maintenance, demolition and dismantling works, industrial installations, and project management services. The company is committed to delivering safe, cost-effective, and innovative solutions while maintaining the highest standards of quality, safety, and environmental responsibility.

Essar Engineering, with ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001 and BizSafe Star Certifications, registered with the Building and Construction Authority (BCA) under the following workheads and builder licences:

  • CW01 – General Building (Grade C1)
  • ME11 – Mechanical Engineering (Grade L2)
  • General Builder Class 2 (GB2)
  • Specialist Builder (Structural Steelwork) – SB(SS)

These registrations reflect the company's capability to undertake a broad spectrum of construction, structural steelwork, and mechanical engineering projects while complying with Singapore's stringent regulatory and technical requirements. Essar Engineering also maintains a strong commitment to workplace safety, operational excellence, and continuous improvement, enabling the company to successfully deliver projects of varying scales and complexities.

Today, Essar Engineering continues to build long-term relationships with clients by providing dependable engineering solutions, professional project execution, and exceptional service from concept through completion.
