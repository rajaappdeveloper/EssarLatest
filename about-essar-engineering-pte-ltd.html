Who We Are

About Our Company

In the year 2007; an enthusiastic energetic professional engineer, post graduated from Singapore with vast experience in Projects handling, Fabrication and Construction industry after an initial period from General Electric (GE), has expanded this company from a Small machine shop to a Medium level Fabrication industry.

During these years, ESSAR acquired its BCA Registration (L1- Mechanical Engineering & GB2 - General Builders) with BizSafe 3 Certification. Now, ESSAR is going for ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001 and BizSafe Star Certification to enhance its competency and participation for major orders in the coming years.

Our History & Achievements

2007 – 2010

Foundation in Machine Shop Operations

Essar Engineering began its journey as a conventional machine shop, providing precision machining and basic engineering services. This period laid the strong technical foundation and core capabilities of the company.

2010 – 2013

Expansion into Fabrication Works

The company expanded its operations into high-quality fabrication works, serving both the Process and Construction industry sectors in Singapore. This phase marked the growth of our engineering capabilities and increasing industry recognition, alongside initial BCA accreditation milestones.

2013 – 2016

BCA Accreditation & Project Expansion

Essar Engineering achieved formal BCA accreditation and successfully transitioned into larger and more complex projects. Key accomplishments during this period include:

  • Erection and commissioning works for construction projects and public sector agencies such as NEA and Singapore Naval Base
  • Specialized projects for leading academic institutions including NUS and NTU
  • Support for NUS research initiatives, including installation and commissioning of advanced equipment at Van Kleef Research Centre
2016 – 2018

Entry into Specialized Engineering Works

The company expanded into specialized engineering sectors, including cleanroom-related works and precision installation projects. This period strengthened our capability in controlled environment projects requiring high technical standards and compliance.

2018 – 2025

Large-Scale Construction & Turnkey Projects

Essar Engineering evolved into a full-fledged main contractor delivering large-scale and turnkey construction solutions. Key capabilities and achievements include:

  • End-to-end project execution including erection, testing, and commissioning
  • Exotic and specialized metal fabrication works
  • Unique engineering projects such as train refurbishment and structural support materials
  • Design-and-build projects with integrated engineering solutions
  • Comprehensive project management services from planning to completion

Today

Essar Engineering continues to grow as a trusted engineering and construction partner in Singapore, delivering innovative, safe, and high-quality solutions across civil, structural, architectural, mechanical, and electrical engineering sectors.
