Our History & Achievements

2007 - 2010

Machine Shop Operations

Conventional Machine shop operations established, laying down the core foundation for engineering services.

2010 - 2013

Fabrication Expansion

Expanded activities into high-quality fabrication works for both the Process and Construction Industry segments in Singapore.

2013 - 2016

BCA Accreditation & Large Projects

Achieved BCA accreditation and successfully ventured into large-scale projects, including:

  • Accomplished erection and commissioning works for Construction Industry and Local Government bodies (NEA, Singapore Naval base).
  • Specialized projects for prime educational institutions including NUS and NTU.
  • Supported NUS Team for Installation and Commissioning of Topo-Scanner at Van Kleef Research Center.
2016 - 2018

Advanced Systems & R&D

Ventured into high-tech semi-conductor clean rooms and advanced R&D systems:

  • Supported Ngee Ann Poly (R&D) in design & development of cooling tower systems.
  • Maintenance of process plants and upgrading of Clean Room ducts in the semi-conductor industry.
  • Implemented SS 316 ducts with special Teflon coating for corrosive solvent exhaust handling.
